💧 Consumer Knowledge

Electricity Billing Guide

A step-by-step handbook on how power meters compute units, how regional rates apply, and how to verify your billing statements.

1. How Meters Read Units

Your electronic meter records electricity consumption in kilowatt-hours (kWh), where 1 unit = 1 kWh (1000 watts used for 1 hour). Net consumption is calculated as Current Reading minus Previous Reading.

2. Regional Unit Rates

State Electricity Regulatory Commissions (SERC) set tariffs based on infrastructure costs, generation fuel mix, and distribution losses. In VoltEdge, you can specify your exact local rate per unit (default: ₹6.00).

3. Fixed Grid Maintenance

A fixed charge (standard ₹50.00) is levied on each bill cycle to maintain high-voltage transformers, power sub-stations, and 24/7 grid reliability regardless of units consumed.

4. Thermal Receipts & PDFs

VoltEdge features a simulated thermal receipt printer with interactive feed sounds and zigzag tear-off animations. Once printed, you can download a certified vector PDF with scannable barcode.
